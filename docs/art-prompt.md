Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE production-ready transparent sprite atlas for the top-down 2D space shooter Alien Invasion. Create a square 1024x1024 RGBA sprite sheet aligned to an EXACT 4 column by 4 row grid of 256x256 cells. There are NO drawn grid lines, labels, text, letters, numbers, shadows on background, or background scenery. All objects are isolated with a truly transparent background. Every single object is centered exactly in its designated cell, with generous 30 px transparent padding to all edges. Cohesive polished sci-fi pixel-art look, metallic shaded hulls, crisp readable silhouettes, bright restrained emissive accents. Ships face UP in orthographic top view. Row 1: cell 1 cyan human player fighter, cell 2 small coral alien scout, cell 3 amber alien gunship with twin cannons, cell 4 broad violet armored alien heavy ship. Row 2: cells 1 AND 2 together form one single wide symmetrical violet alien mothership boss centered at x256,y384 occupying the 512x256 region; cell 3 small gray asteroid; cell 4 larger rugged gray asteroid. Row 3: cell 1 health supply icon, emerald capsule with white plus; cell 2 missile supply icon, amber diamond with white rocket silhouette; cell 3 lightning supply icon, violet hexagon with white lightning bolt; cell 4 orbital support icon, cyan circular emblem with three white descending streaks. Row 4: cell 1 thin cyan crosshair reticle; cell 2 thin amber square target-lock corners; cell 3 compact golden orange explosion burst with transparent outer edges; cell 4 pair of upward glowing cyan pulse bullets. The exact placement, different silhouettes, consistent style, transparency and clean cell separation are essential. No perspective angle, no scenes, no typography.
